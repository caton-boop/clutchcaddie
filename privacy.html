Privacy policy

Last updated: November 2026

This is a plain-language starting draft. Have it reviewed before launch, and update it when you add analytics, ads or accounts.

What stays on your device

Your game progress, daily results, weekly round in progress, streaks and the name you last used are stored in your browser's local storage. We also create a random player ID on your device so the leaderboard knows which score is yours. You can clear all of this at any time by clearing this site's data in your browser.

What we store on our servers

When you choose to post a score, we store the name you typed, your score, your clutch rating, your random player ID and the time you posted. Names and scores are shown publicly on the leaderboard. We do not ask for your email, real name or location to play.

Service providers

The site is hosted by our hosting provider and leaderboard data is stored with our database provider. They process data only to run the service.

Advertising and analytics

If we show ads or use analytics, those partners may use cookies or similar technologies to measure usage and show ads. Where required, we will ask for your consent first, and you can change your choice at any time.

Children

Clutch Caddie is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Removing your scores

To remove a name or score from the leaderboard, contact us at hello@clutchcaddie.com and include the name and date.

Changes

We will update this page when our practices change and change the date above.
